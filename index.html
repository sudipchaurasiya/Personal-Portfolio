About Me

My name is Sudip Kumar Chaurasiya. I am a Bachelor of Information Technology (BIT) student with a strong interest in cybersecurity and ethical hacking. I enjoy learning how computer systems, networks, and web applications work and how they can be protected from security threats.

I am interested in areas such as penetration testing, network security, web security, reconnaissance, vulnerability assessment, and security monitoring. I like learning through practical lab exercises because they help me understand cybersecurity concepts better.

My goal is to continue improving my cybersecurity knowledge, develop strong technical skills, and build a career in the cybersecurity field. I believe that continuous learning and practical experience are important for becoming a good cybersecurity professional.
